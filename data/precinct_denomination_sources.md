# Presbyterian polling-site labels

The 2024 Florida official precinct export supplies the county and polling-site name. The county-and-code overrides in `scripts/build_fl_precinct_friendly_names.py` add a denomination only when a church or denomination source identifies that same site. Labels use `Church Name, DENOMINATION`.

- **Alachua:** [Faith Presbyterian in Gainesville](https://faithgainesville.org/contact) identifies PCA. The PCUSA congregation directory identifies Gainesville's [Westminster](https://pcusa.org/congregation/westminster-church-gainesville-fl), [Covenant](https://pcusa.org/congregation/covenant-church-gainesville-fl), and [Kanapaha](https://pcusa.org/congregation/kanapaha-church-gainesville-fl).
- **Clay:** [First Presbyterian in Green Cove Springs](https://fpcgreencovesprings.org/welcome_to_worship_) identifies PCUSA.
- **Hernando:** [Grace Presbyterian in Spring Hill](https://www.gracespringhill.com/who-we-are.html) identifies PCUSA. Faith's [PCA pastor search profile](https://www.pcaac.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/SeniorPastor_FaithBrooksvilleFL.pdf) records its move from EPC to PCA. The label uses its current name, Faith Church, PCA, despite the older polling export's Faith Evangelical Presbyterian Church wording.
- **Lee:** [Faith Presbyterian in Cape Coral](https://pcusa.org/congregation/faith-church-cape-coral-fl) is in the PCUSA directory. [Peace River Presbytery](https://peaceriverpresbytery.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/30/2025/02/Stated.Meeting.Reports.Feb-20.2025.pdf) identifies Buckingham Presbyterian in Fort Myers.
- **Leon:** [Wildwood's church site](https://www.wildwoodtlh.com/) identifies PCA. The PCUSA directory or congregation sites identify [Faith](https://pcusa.org/congregation/faith-church-tallahassee-fl), [Fellowship](https://fellowshippcusa.org/), [Lafayette](https://www.fountainchurchtallahassee.org/about-us.html), and [Christ](https://pcusa.org/congregation/christ-church-tallahassee-fl).
- **Monroe:** The [ECO Florida church directory](https://eco-pres.org/presbyteries/presbytery-of-florida/?page=2) lists Kirk of the Keys at the Marathon polling-site address.
- **Seminole:** The PCUSA directory identifies [Markham Woods](https://pcusa.org/congregation/markham-woods-church-lake-mary-fl), [Wekiva](https://pcusa.org/congregation/wekiva-church-longwood-fl), and [Oviedo](https://pcusa.org/congregation/oviedo-church-oviedo-fl). [Covenant's own site](https://cpcoviedo.com/beliefs/) identifies it as PCA and gives the same 1925 County Road 419 address as [precinct 73 on the county polling-place list](https://irp.cdn-website.com/bc68dbc5/files/uploaded/2024_Polling_Locations_-_Signs_2024-159890f7.pdf).
